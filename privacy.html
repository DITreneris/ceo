Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Free tool

Form inputs and saved sessions are stored in your browser (localStorage) only. We do not upload your operating data to our servers when you use the free generator.

Analytics

We may use privacy-focused analytics (e.g., Vercel Web Analytics) to understand aggregate traffic. We do not collect personal business metrics from your forms.

Paid PDF purchases

When you buy a PDF guide:

  • Stripe processes payment and may send you a receipt.
  • Resend delivers your download email.
  • Upstash Redis stores fulfillment state and signed download tokens (session id, product, email).
  • Vercel Blob hosts private PDF files; downloads are served through signed URLs.
