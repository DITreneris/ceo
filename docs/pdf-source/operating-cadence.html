Chapter 1Prompts

Daily prompts — Fast and Deep

Replace [brackets]. Add DEPTH and OUTPUT FORMAT lines from the cheat sheet on page 8.

Daily · Fast5 min
ROLE: You are an operations analyst helping CEO/COO evaluate yesterday.

YESTERDAY DATA:
- Revenue: [$amount]
- New clients / leads: [count or description]
- Expenses: [$amount]

KEY EVENTS: [events]

QUESTION: What should I do differently today based on yesterday data?

DEPTH: Answer briefly and clearly: max 3 points, each 1-2 sentences, no intro.

OUTPUT FORMAT: 3 priorities + 3 actions + 1 risk
Daily · Deep15 min
Same ROLE and YESTERDAY DATA as above.

DEPTH: Provide deep analysis with context and reasoning. For each point explain why it matters and impact. Use numbers.

OUTPUT FORMAT: 3 priorities (with rationale) + 5 actions (with timelines) + 2 risks (with mitigation) + 1 long-term recommendation

Chapter 3Reflection

CEO reflection (weekly)

End the week with fact-based coaching—not generic advice. Use after your weekly numbers review.

You are my strategic coach. Help me run a fact-based weekly CEO reflection.

Context:
- Weekly goal: [goal]
- Key decisions: [decisions]
- What went well: [strengths]
- Where I got stuck: [weaknesses]
- Team signals: [facts]
- Financial outcome: [revenue / expenses / margin]

Answer in 4 parts:
1. 5 precise questions I avoid but must answer
2. 3 main CEO decision risks in this situation
3. 3 decisions for next week with clear priority (A/B/C)
4. One personal 7-day discipline with biggest impact

RulesDiscipline

Economic discipline (top 5 rules)

Every prompt in this playbook should follow these principles.

  • Cash flow > profit > revenue — this is the decision sequence.
  • Runway < 6 months = red zone; require an action plan.
  • Only 3 priorities per week — not ten.
  • Each action needs an owner and deadline.
  • Weekly review: what worked, what failed, what changes next.

Full rule set in AI Operations Center → Economic discipline rules. Methodology: Meta · Input · Output · Reasoning · Quality · Advanced (Prompt Anatomy).

PracticeTechniques

Techniques that work for CEOs

1

Threads, not one-offs

Keep one chat per week’s operating review. Re-paste updated numbers; AI retains context.

2

Edit before you act

Edit the generated prompt in the tool output area, then copy to ChatGPT or Claude.

3

Saved sessions

Use Save session in AI Operations Center to reload last week’s inputs (browser only).

4

Clarify, then create

Ask AI to challenge your assumptions before drafting board or investor text.

Do not paste customer PII, full cap tables, unreleased financials, HR disciplinary records, or attorney-privileged material into consumer AI tools.
