StrategicQuestions

Strategic questions bank

Copy one question into the TASK line of any master prompt. Categorized by executive job.

Strategy & priorities

  • What are the 3 highest-leverage priorities this quarter given our cash and pipeline?
  • Which initiative should we stop, start, or continue — and why?
  • Where are we spreading too thin relative to runway?
  • What single metric best predicts our goal this quarter?

Finance & unit economics

  • Is our LTV/CAC ratio healthy for our stage — what would improve it fastest?
  • Where is margin leaking — product, delivery, or acquisition?
  • What happens to runway if revenue flatlines for 90 days?
  • Which cost line is most defensible to cut without breaking growth?

Board & investors

  • What should the board hear in one page — facts only?
  • What are the top 3 risks we are not discussing enough?
  • What decision needs board input vs CEO-only this cycle?

Risk & governance

  • What early-warning indicators should trigger a Type 1 review?
  • Where is customer or revenue concentration unacceptable?

Growth & pricing

  • Which growth lever has best impact × confidence × speed?
  • Should we test a price increase — what criteria prove success?

CONTEXT:

- Revenue model: [subscription / project / mixed]
- Strategic objective: [e.g. reach $100K MRR by Q3]

- Constraints: [cash runway months, headcount cap, key deadline]

FACTS:
- Revenue (month): [$amount]
- Expenses (month): [$amount]

- Cash balance: [$amount]
- Runway (months): [number or unknown]
- Pipeline summary: [weighted value, stage mix]

MasterBoard

Master prompt — Board

STRATEGIC · Board30 min
ROLE:
You are a board-reporting advisor preparing a factual executive summary for a US private company board or investor update.

CONTEXT:
- Company type: [SaaS / services / hybrid]
- Reporting period: [month / quarter ending date]
- Board meeting date: [date]
- Strategic objective: [company goal this period]
- Constraints: [runway, fundraising status, material events]

FACTS:
- Revenue (period): [$amount] vs plan [$amount]
- Expenses (period): [$amount] vs plan [$amount]
- Profit / burn (period): [$amount]
- Cash balance: [$amount]
- Runway (months): [base case — label assumptions]
- Pipeline / ARR / retention: [metrics with dates]
- Key wins, losses, hires, product milestones: [facts only]
- Known risks: [specific, dated where possible]

TASK:
Draft a one-page board-ready memo: financial status, top priorities, KPI actions, and risk matrix. Facts and numbers only — no unsupported opinions.

REASONING RULES:
- Separate facts, assumptions, and recommendations.
- Do not invent metrics. Mark “data not provided” instead of guessing.
- Formal tone suitable for external directors.
- Every priority links to a measurable KPI or dated milestone.
- Flag items requiring CFO verification before distribution.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
1. Executive summary (max 3 sentences, facts only)
2. Financial status table: metric | actual | plan | variance | note
3. Top 3 strategic priorities with owner and target date
4. 5 actions with KPIs: action | owner | deadline | success metric
5. Risk matrix: risk | probability (L/M/H) | impact (L/M/H) | mitigation | owner
6. Board recommendation: [one clear ask or “no board action required”]
7. CFO review required: list every number and assumption needing finance sign-off
8. CEO decision required: [approve for board / revise / hold]

QUALITY GATE:
If period actuals or cash balance are missing, ask up to 5 clarifying questions before producing the memo. Do not publish until CFO review gate is complete.

BoardWorkflow

Board workflow + checklist

Run 5–7 days before the meeting. CEO owns narrative; CFO owns numbers.

  1. T-7 days: COO/CFO close books; export actuals vs plan.
  2. T-5 days: CEO runs Master Board prompt with FACTS block complete.
  3. T-4 days: CFO review gate — verify every number, variance, and runway assumption.
  4. T-3 days: CEO edits tone and priorities; map risks to owners and dates.
  5. T-2 days: Optional: send draft to lead director for factual corrections only.
  6. T-1 day: Final sign-off logged in output registry (page 25).

Board memo checklist

  • All numbers match finance source (not AI-generated)
  • Variances vs plan explained in one line each
  • Max 3 priorities — each with owner and date
  • Risk matrix has probability, impact, mitigation, owner
  • CFO sign-off recorded with date
  • No customer PII, cap table detail, or unreleased material
  • CEO explicit decision: approve / revise / hold
CEO decision required: Does this memo need a board vote, information only, or an executive-session topic?

Strategic shortcuts

Six decisions — 2 minutes each. Paste directly; pair with full prompts for deep work.

Runway snapshot · 2 min

When: Pre-board or monthly close. Need a fast cash read.

Cash: $[X]. Monthly burn: $[X]. Revenue trend: [up/flat/down].
Give me runway (months), one risk flag, and one action.
No invented numbers.

→ Output: runway estimate · one risk · one action

LTV/CAC check · 2 min

When: Before pricing change or fundraise deck.

LTV: $[X]. CAC: $[X]. Payback: [mo].
Is my LTV:CAC healthy for SaaS? Name one lever to improve it.
Flag if data is insufficient.

→ Output: ratio read · one improvement lever

Top 3 risks · 2 min

When: Quarterly review or before board meeting.

Company: [type]. Top client: [% ARR]. Key dependency: [name].
List my top 3 risks with P×I (L/M/H) and one owner each.
Do not invent incidents.

→ Output: 3 risks · P×I · owners

Growth lever pick · 2 min

When: After scoring matrix (p.18) — quick decision.

Top 2 levers by score: [name, score] and [name, score].
Which one should I start this month? One-paragraph reason.
Include one quick win (<2 weeks) for each.

→ Output: recommendation · reason · quick win

CEO reflection · 2 min

When: End of week or quarter close.

This [week/quarter]: [2-3 key facts].
Give me 3 facts vs 1 assumption I'm making.
One behavior change for next period. Owner: CEO.

→ Output: fact/assumption split · one behavior change

Flag if LTV data is missing.
