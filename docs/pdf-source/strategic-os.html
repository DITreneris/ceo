Master prompt — Fast

ROLE: You are a strategic business consultant working with CEO/COO.

CONTEXT:
- Strategic objective: [$100K MRR by Q2]
- Time horizon: [This quarter]

FINANCE:
- Revenue (month): [$45,000]
- Expenses (month): [$38,000]
- Profit (month): [$7,000]
- Cash balance: [$120,000]
- Runway (months): [16]

FACTS: [key facts]

QUESTION: [your question]

DEPTH: Answer briefly: max 3 points, 1-2 sentences each, no intro.
OUTPUT FORMAT: 3 priorities + 3 actions + 1 risk

Data hygiene and governance-lite ownership

Governance-lite means clear owners and review gates—not a full enterprise AI program.

  • AI Operations Center stores sessions in localStorage only—not on our servers.
  • Do not paste cap tables, customer lists, or unreleased earnings into public AI.
  • Verify every number AI cites before board or investor use.
  • Owner matrix: CEO signs board/investor text; COO signs operating summaries; CFO signs finance tables.
  • Document approver + date on any external-facing AI-assisted PDF or email.
