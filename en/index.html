            Prompt Anatomy 6-Block Executive Framework

            Every prompt generated in the AI Operations Center structures executive context across six functional blocks so ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini returns a decision-ready brief rather than generic advice:

            1. Meta (Role & Decision Mandate)
            Sets the executive lens (CEO, COO, or Board advisor), operating cadence, and decision horizon.
            2. Input (KPIs, Baseline & Constraints)
            Anchors weekly revenue, costs, cash balance, runway, active project status, and pipeline facts.
            3. Output (Decision-Ready Format)
            Enforces a 1-page brief layout where every action names an Owner, Deadline, and target KPI.
            4. Reasoning (Trade-offs & Bottlenecks)
            Requires root-cause diagnosis of delivery or pipeline bottlenecks before proposing new initiatives.
            5. Quality (Fact vs. Assumption Verification)
            Separates stated metrics from estimates and flags missing numbers to confirm before acting.
            6. Advanced (Risk & Scenario Stress-Test)
            Surfaces the single highest-probability execution risk and its mitigation for leadership sign-off.

            Worked Example: Weekly KPI Input to Reviewed CEO Brief

            1. Sample Executive Input (Weekly Mode)

            Weekly data: Revenue $310k (vs. $340k plan); Costs $365k; Net burn $55k/week; Cash $2.75M (~11.5 months runway at current burn).
            Active projects: Enterprise onboarding v2 delayed 9 days by the customer’s SOC 2 security questionnaire; Q4 pricing tier rollout on track.
            Pipeline: $1.15M weighted; 3 late-stage renewals ($180k ARR) awaiting SLA sign-off.
            Question: Which projects do we prioritize and what are the top 3 actions this week?

            2. Reviewed Brief Output (Fast depth)

            Missing data to confirm: Aged AR over 45 days; churn risk on the 3 renewals.
            1. Close the 3 renewals ($180k ARR): COO + Legal finalize SLA redlines | by Thursday noon | KPI: 3 of 3 signed by Friday.
            2. Unblock Enterprise onboarding v2: VP Eng + Security Lead close open SOC 2 questionnaire items | by Wednesday 5 PM | KPI: go-live slip held at 9 days.
            3. Cut net burn: CFO freezes net-new contractor spend | by Monday EOD | KPI: net burn under $45k/week until revenue is back on plan.
            Top risk: A renewal stalls on SLA terms and its ARR slips past quarter end. Mitigation: CEO calls each renewal’s executive sponsor by Wednesday.

            These rules follow Prompt Anatomy's 6-block methodology: Meta · Input · Output · Reasoning · Quality · Advanced — designed by Tomas Staniulis (Founder, Prompt Anatomy · AI governance & executive operations).
