# PDF Content V02

Planning source for upgrading the two paid CEO PDF products from v1.1 into a stronger v2 content system.

This document is for humans and agents before editing:

- `docs/pdf-source/operating-cadence.html`
- `docs/pdf-source/strategic-os.html`

Use this as the content blueprint. Do not start by redesigning covers. Upgrade the internal product value first.

## 1. Product Verdict

Current state: **PARTIAL**.

The two PDFs are useful but not yet premium executive playbooks. They are structured, readable, and commercially plausible at the current entry prices, but they still feel closer to executive prompt guides than full paid products.

| Product | Current Price | Verdict | Buyer Satisfaction Risk |
|---|---:|---|---|
| CEO AI Operations Playbook | `$9.99` | Thin but usable | Medium |
| CEO AI Strategy Playbook | `$19.99` | Stronger, but underdeveloped | Medium |

### Operations Playbook

The Operations PDF gives a practical daily and weekly cadence. It is easy to understand and can help a CEO or COO run a Monday review. The risk is that it is only 12 pages and lacks enough examples, templates, meeting tools, and implementation support.

Keep it as the entry playbook, but make it feel like a working operating tool.

### Strategy Playbook

The Strategy PDF has the right premium topics: board prompts, ROI, maturity diagnosis, unit economics, runway, risk, governance, and implementation planning. The weakness is depth. Several pages are one-page skeletons instead of complete executive assets.

Keep it as the flagship playbook, but add completed examples, stronger workflows, and decision tools.

V02 should preserve the current product positioning:

- EN-US only.

- `/lt/` remains legacy/regression only.
- Launch scope stays focused on the two PDFs.

## 3. Non-Negotiable Content Standards

Every V02 page must pass these checks.

| Standard | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Buyer job | The page must solve one specific CEO/COO/CFO job. |
| Practical output | The page must produce a decision, checklist, prompt, template, scorecard, agenda, or action plan. |
| Owner | Where relevant, name who owns the output: CEO, COO, CFO, leadership team. |
| Time horizon | Clarify daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, board, or 90-day use. |
| Copy-paste utility | Prompts must be complete enough to use without “same as above” references. |
| Quality gate | Prompts and workflows must include review rules, missing-data handling, and human sign-off. |
| Commercial value | The page must increase speed, decision quality, risk control, delegation, or implementation clarity. |
| Scanability | A busy executive must understand the page in under 30 seconds. |

Remove or compress pages that only explain concepts without producing an executive artifact.

## 6. Prompt Quality Standard

All V02 prompts should follow this pattern unless there is a strong reason not to.

```text
ROLE:
You are a [specific executive-grade role] advising a [CEO/COO/CFO].

CONTEXT:
- Company type:
- Revenue model:
- Current goal:
- Decision horizon:
- Constraints:

FACTS:
- Revenue:
- Costs:
- Cash:
- Pipeline:
- Team capacity:
- Key events:
- Known risks:

TASK:
[Specific decision or analysis request.]

REASONING RULES:
- Separate facts, assumptions, and recommendations.
- Do not invent missing numbers.
- Flag missing data before recommending action.
- Prioritize cash, runway, customer impact, execution capacity, and reversibility.
- For each recommendation, explain the business reason in one sentence.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
1. Executive summary, max 5 lines
2. Decision table: priority, rationale, expected impact, owner, deadline
3. Risks and mitigations
4. Data needed before commitment
5. CEO decision required

QUALITY GATE:
If the input is too vague, ask up to 5 clarifying questions before producing the final answer.
```

### Prompt Classification Rules

Use this when auditing existing prompts.

| Classification | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Keep | Already specific, useful, and executive-ready. | Only polish wording. |
| Improve | Good base, but missing constraints or quality gate. | Add missing criteria. |
| Rewrite | Too generic, shorthand, or incomplete. | Replace with full prompt. |
| Remove | Does not support buyer job or duplicates another prompt. | Cut or merge. |

### Minimum Prompt Acceptance Criteria

A prompt is V02-ready only if:

- Role is specific.
- Task is specific.
- Inputs are clear.
- Output format is structured.
- Quality criteria are explicit.
- It tells AI what not to invent.
- It gives the CEO a decision-ready output.
- A non-technical executive can use it without extra explanation.
